Smart Features Built for Modern Kitchens

Everything you need to organize inventory, track expiry dates, plan meals, and eliminate food waste — all synced in real time.

📡

NFC Zone Tags

Tap a tag on your fridge, freezer, or pantry shelf. Instantly see what's in that zone and mark what you took — no opening the app, no hunting through lists.

📦

Pantry Tracking

Add items by barcode scan or manually. Track quantities, expiry dates, and get alerts before food goes bad. Never throw out forgotten groceries again.

🤖

AI Meal Planning

Generate a full week of meal plans based on what's actually in your pantry. No more "what's for dinner?" — the AI sees your inventory and plans around it.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Sharing

Invite up to 4 household members. Everyone sees the same pantry in real time — no more duplicate grocery runs because someone didn't know you already bought it.

📷

Barcode Scanner

Scan any grocery barcode and get the product name, brand, and calories pulled in automatically. Adding groceries after a shopping trip takes seconds.

🍽️

Recipe Suggestions

AI-generated recipes built from your current pantry. Save favorites, plan around dietary needs, and always cook with what you already have on hand.

🛒

Smart Shopping List

Items that run out auto-appear on your shopping list. Add anything manually, check things off at the store, and keep the whole household in sync.

🧾

Cart Total + Tax Tracker

See a running total of your cart as you shop, with tax calculated in real time. No more checkout surprises — know exactly what you're spending before you hit the register.
